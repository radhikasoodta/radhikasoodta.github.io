How I lead

People first, AI-enabled

Technology handles the volume. People make every decision that matters.

Every candidate is a person.

Respectful, timely and honest at every step, whether or not they get the offer.

Hiring plans are business plans.

Headcount is set with the CEO and CFO against revenue and runway.

Evidence over instinct.

Scorecards make decisions fairer, faster and easier to stand behind.

AI for the groundwork.

Research, first drafts and reporting, with outreach about 30% faster. A person reviews everything that reaches a candidate.
