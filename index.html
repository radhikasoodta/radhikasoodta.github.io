Perspective

How I think about talent

The principles I bring to every leadership table.

The hiring plan is the business plan.

Headcount decisions belong with the CEO and the CFO, set against revenue and runway, not negotiated one requisition at a time.

The best candidates are not applying.

Great teams are built by going out and finding people, then earning their attention with a compelling case and a respectful process.

Evidence over instinct.

Structured interviews and calibrated scorecards make hiring fairer, faster and easier to defend, and they widen the slate on their own.

AI does the groundwork. People make the call.

AI handles research, drafting and reporting so a lean team spends its time on judgment and relationships, with human review on everything that reaches a candidate.
